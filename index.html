01 Founder · My own AI engine

Swaylo AI

Most messages don't need a big model, so most messages don't get one.

Swaylo classifies every message the moment it arrives and routes it to the leanest model that can actually answer it. A small fast model for ordinary text, which is nearly all the traffic. One that can hear for a voice note. One that can see for a photo, even a delivery address written by hand. It switches inside a single conversation, and three models still sound like one assistant with one memory.

  • Fails over between two providers mid sentence, so an outage degrades the answer instead of ending it.
  • Ten Indian languages, in their own script or in Latin letters.
  • No keyword lists. No menu trees. It judges the message.
