Before you buy

An honest read

What it has going for it

  • A recognisable high-street brand in a price bracket where most options are unbranded.
  • All-black dial and strap is about as easy to match as a watch gets — office, casual, evening.
  • Quartz movement means no winding and years between battery changes.
  • Over 1.07 lakh of the 1.25 lakh ratings are 4 or 5 stars, which is a lot of buyers to be wrong.

What to keep in mind

  • The case is brass, not stainless steel — expect fashion-watch build, not a heirloom.
  • "Water resistant" here means splashes and rain. Do not swim or shower in it.
  • The dial is 33 mm — Flipkart's own size chart puts it well under a credit card's width. That is a slim, dressy size, not the chunky 40 mm+ look.
  • Metal link straps usually need a link or two removed to fit; a local watch shop does this in minutes.
  • About 1 rating in 20 is a single star. Read a few of those on Flipkart before you order.
