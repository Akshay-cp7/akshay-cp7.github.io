An honest read

What it has going for it

  • The dial is the whole point — a gold five-spoke rim over a drilled brake disc and caliper, and it spins rather than sitting still.
  • Black case and bracelet with gold as the only accent, which is why a loud idea still reads as wearable.
  • 43 mm across and 13 mm thick — the chunky, fills-the-wrist look, not a dress watch.
  • Buyers rate design and features 4.7 and look and feel 4.6 — the highest scores of any watch on this site.
  • A real 1 year warranty, and the seller publishes a customer care number and address rather than hiding behind a brand name.
  • 30 m water resistance, so rain, splashes and hand-washing are covered.

What to keep in mind

  • Flipkart lists this as an "Analog-Digital" watch with an alarm, chronograph, date display and light. Every product photo shows a plain analog watch with no digital panel and no date window. Treat that spec row as copy-paste, and buy it as an analog watch.
  • The listing also gives the dial colour as "Black, Green". It is black and gold in every image.
  • The case is zinc alloy with a black coating, not steel. Plating is explicitly excluded from the warranty, as is the battery.
  • 1 rating in 7 is a single star — 12 of 84 — which is a high floor for a 4.0 average, and 84 ratings is a small sample.
  • 30 m means rain and hand-washing. The brand's own card rules out shower, swimming, snorkelling and diving.
  • The bracelet is 200 mm long and 20 mm wide, so most wrists will need a couple of links taken out — a local watch shop does it in minutes.
  • At ₹1,302 this is the most expensive of the three wheel-dial watches here, and the discount off MRP is the smallest. You are paying for the warranty and the finish.
