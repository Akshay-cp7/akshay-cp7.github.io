Before you buy

An honest read

What it has going for it

  • The spinner is the whole point — a red star over a dark wheel that turns a full 360° behind the hands. Under ₹500 there is not much else doing this.
  • Black case, black bracelet, red dial: the colour is confined to one thing, which is why it works.
  • 42 mm across, 12 mm thick and 140 g — a chunky, fills-the-wrist watch, not a dress piece.
  • Case and bracelet are both stainless steel here, not the plated alloy usual at this price, and the listing claims scratch resistance.
  • Quartz movement: no winding, and years between battery changes.
  • 99 of the 127 ratings are 4 or 5 stars, and look and feel is the highest-scoring parameter at 4.1 — which is what you would buy it for.

What to keep in mind

  • There is no warranty. Flipkart's warranty summary for this listing reads "na" — not a duration, not a service type. Nothing.
  • No water resistance is claimed anywhere on the listing either. Treat it as a dry-wear watch: no rain, no hand-washing with it on.
  • The manufacturer and packer are both listed as "ramani innovative Gujrat" — spelling theirs. There is no brand service network behind this.
  • Value for money is the weakest score buyers give it, 3.6, well below the 4.1 they give the looks.
  • 127 ratings is a small sample, and about 1 in 12 of them is a single star. Read those before you order.
  • 140 g on the wrist is heavy, and the bracelet will need a link or two taken out at a local watch shop.
  • Telling the time takes a glance longer than usual — the spinner fills the middle, and the hands read against the outer ring.
  • The ₹2,599 MRP exists mainly to make "81% off" possible. Judge it on the ₹486 you actually pay.
