PDF & Images to Booklet

Privacy Policy

Effective 27 July 2026

Your documents never leave your phone. Booklets are built entirely on your own device, and the PDFs and images you open in this app are never uploaded to any server.

Who this covers

This policy covers the mobile app PDF & Images to Booklet, published by Akshay CP on the Apple App Store and Google Play. It explains what the app does and does not collect.

Your files

When you pick a PDF or a set of images, the app reads them, rearranges the pages, and writes a new booklet PDF. All of this happens on your device using its own processor and storage.

Your files are not sent to me, and they are not sent to anyone else. I have no way to see them. Recent booklets are listed inside the app purely so you can find them again, and that list is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve those ads, AdMob may access your device's advertising identifier — the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS, or the Advertising ID on Android — along with general information about your device, such as its model, operating system version, language, rough location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads.

This information is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy.

Tracking, and how to turn it off

On iOS, the app asks your permission before any tracking happens. If you decline, your advertising identifier is not used to track you across other apps and websites. You will still see ads, they will just be less relevant. You can change your mind at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings under Google › Ads.

Buying the one-time Remove Ads & Watermark purchase stops ads being shown and stops the advertising identifier being used for advertising in this app.

Purchases

The app offers a single one-time purchase that removes ads and removes the watermark from the booklets you create. Payments are processed by the store you installed the app from — Apple on iOS, or Google Play Billing on Android. I never see or store your card details. The app only records, on your own device, whether the purchase was made.

What I collect

I do not run any server for this app, and I do not operate accounts, logins, or analytics of my own. I do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or documents. The only data leaving your device is what Google AdMob collects to show ads, as described above.

Children

This app is a general-purpose printing utility and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please get in touch and I will look into it.

Permissions

  • Files and photos — used only to read the PDF or images you choose, and to save the booklet where you ask.
  • Tracking (iOS) — optional, asked once, and used only for advertising.
  • Internet — used to load ads and to complete purchases. It is not used to upload your documents.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Meaningful changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about anything the app does, are welcome at contact.akshaycp@gmail.com.
