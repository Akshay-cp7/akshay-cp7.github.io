Support

Updated 31 July 2026

Something not working, or an idea for what an app should do next? Write to me directly — it comes straight to my inbox and I answer every message myself.

contact.akshaycp@gmail.com

To get to an answer faster, it helps if you mention which app you are using, your phone or tablet model, and what you did just before things went wrong. A screenshot is worth a lot.

Common questions

I paid to remove ads, but they are still showing

Open the app and look for the restore purchases option on the same screen where you bought it. Purchases are tied to the store account that made them, so make sure the device is signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you used. If it still does not come back, email me with your receipt and I will sort it out.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store, not by me. On iPhone and iPad, use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use the order history in the Google Play app. Tell me what went wrong as well — if it is a bug on my side I would rather fix it.

Where do my files go?

Nowhere. The apps do their work on your own device and do not upload your documents, photos or notes to any server of mine — I do not run one. The full details are in the privacy policy for each app.

I found a bug, or I want a feature added

Please send it over. Most of what gets built next comes from these emails, and small fixes often ship within a week or two.

Response time

I usually reply within two working days. If a week goes by with no answer, the message probably landed in spam somewhere — send it again and it will get through.
